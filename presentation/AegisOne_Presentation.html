The problem

Phishing works when a person acts, and defences have not kept up

Attacks come from everywhere

Fake login pages, fake websites, QR codes, malicious files and impersonation, not only email.

Most tools watch email only

They cannot stop a password being typed into a fake page, or a file being opened.

Warnings do not explain

Users see "blocked" with no reason, and admins cannot audit why.

Small companies are exposed

Enterprise security is costly, and sending company data to a vendor's cloud is a risk.

Our solution

One AI system that protects people at the moment they act

Detects across channels

Links, pages, email, messages, images and files, all judged by one AI engine.

Explains every decision

A 0 to 100 risk score with reasons in plain words, plus what to do next.

Stays in-house

It runs on the company's own server. No client data goes to us or to anyone else.

Learns from the company

Admin-verified feedback tunes the scores for that organization, safely and reversibly.

The AI engines

Five engines, one decision

Links

URL

Model

DistilBERT on the address, plus 64 address features and a brand-imitation check

Data

137,217 URLs: Kaggle malicious URLs, Tranco, PhishTank, URLhaus. Split by domain.

97.2%own test · 87.5% on 400 unseen URLs
Email

Email

Model

DistilBERT + LoRA, Bi-LSTM, attention, 10 handcrafted signals. 69.9M parameters, 5% trained.

Data

150,000 emails: 75,000 phishing and 75,000 legitimate from five corpora, including Enron and Nazario.

99.4%accuracy · AUC 0.9991
Messages

Text / SMS

Model

Same design, starting from the email model's weights, tuned for short messages

Data

About 10,000 labelled messages: ham, spam and smishing

97.2%F1 on validation data
Pages

Screenshot

Model

EfficientNet-B3 fine-tuned in three phases. OCR sends page text to the other engines.

Data

3,568 screenshots: 1,697 phishing (PhishTank) and 1,871 legitimate (Tranco)

83.5%F1 · AUC 0.880 · supporting signal
Files

Attachments

Method

Opens PDF, Office, HTML and ZIP; finds scripts, macros and password forms; sends text and links to the models

Data

No training. Rules plus the text and URL models.

Risk ≥ 0.5flags the file · macros always flag

Fusion engine combines all signals into one 0 to 100 risk, then a learning layer tunes it for each company.

Features · dashboards

A dashboard for every role

Employee

  • Live risk score and warnings in the browser
  • "Why is this phishing?" in plain words
  • Threat center and history
  • Report a false positive or an incident
  • Manual scan of links, text, images, files
  • Personal security score

Manager

  • Department risk, by employee
  • Incident queue: resolve, escalate, comment
  • Assign awareness training
  • Reports and exports
  • Chat and announcements
  • Own department only

Admin

  • Whole-organization analytics
  • Approve accounts, manage users and departments
  • Rule on incidents
  • AI model status and learning layer
  • Audit log
  • Setup wizard with CSV import
Features · how it works

Protect, explain, learn

1 · In the browser

Protect

  • Every page and link scored 0 to 100
  • Forms stopped before a password is sent
  • Downloads held until scanned
  • Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp Web, pop-ups
  • Right-click to scan anything
2 · On the screen

Explain

  • Warning page in front of the danger
  • Reasons in plain language
  • Advice on what to do next
  • Report a false positive or open an incident
3 · In the dashboards

Learn

  • Manager triages the report
  • Admin gives the official ruling
  • Verified rulings tune the scores, only if provably better
  • Removable at any time
